Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

TableSnap ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is engineered from the ground up as a privacy-first, zero-telemetry tool. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal data, web browsing history, or table contents.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing

All DOM parsing, HTML table harvesting, auto-scroll processing, and CSV generation occur strictly within your local machine's memory in Google Chrome. No tables or data rows are ever dispatched to any remote server or third-party service.

3. Permissions Justification

  • activeTab: Used strictly to read the table elements on the active webpage only when you explicitly open the popup and click "Scan".
  • storage: Used solely to cache your license activation status (Pro state) and language preferences on your local browser.

4. Payment Security

Payment transactions are processed externally through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant partners (Lemon Squeezy / Stripe). TableSnap never sees, stores, or handles your credit card numbers or banking credentials.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data privacy inquiries, please contact us at: support@tablesnap.app.
